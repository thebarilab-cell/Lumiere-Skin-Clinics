FAQ

Answers before you ask.

Still curious? Book a free consultation and we'll walk you through everything, no pressure.

Ask a Specialist →
Is the consultation really free?

Yes. Your first 30-minute skin analysis with a specialist is complimentary and completely no-obligation.

Do treatments have downtime?

Most of our signature treatments have zero downtime. Advanced protocols like resurfacing may have 24–72 hours of gentle recovery — we'll always tell you in advance.

What if I have sensitive skin?

Every protocol is customized after a full analysis. We regularly work with rosacea, eczema, and reactive skin types.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. Our memberships spread cost across the year, and we partner with Affirm for treatment financing.

How soon will I see results?

Radiance treatments show results instantly. Corrective protocols typically show visible change within 3–4 sessions.
